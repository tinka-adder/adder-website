privacy

adder keeps as little as it can, stores it in Ireland, and lets you leave in the way you choose.

Who we are

adder is made by Tinka Claeys and Sophie Keij, two friends in Brussels. While adder is being tested, the two of us are responsible for what happens to your data. There is no company behind it yet, no investors and no one else reading along.

Questions, or something you want changed or removed: ssst@adder.care. One of us answers, usually within a few days.

What we keep and why

WhatWhy
Your name and email addressSo your friends know it’s you, and so we can send your login code

The places you trust, with your words: why, good to know, who it’s good forThat is what adder is for: passing them on to your nest

We use all of this only to make adder work. We don’t sell it, rent it out or use it for advertising.

What we don’t keep

  • Your home address.
  • Where you are. The app uses your location only on your phone, to show places near you. It never reaches us.
  • Your contacts. adder never reads your address book.
  • Passwords. You come in with a code that works once.
  • Advertising or tracking tools. There are none in adder.

Why we ask for your explicit consent

The places you trust can say something about your health. A gynaecologist, a psychologist or an addiction doctor in your list tells a story, even without a word from you. European law treats that as health data, and so do we.

So when you first come in, we ask you separately whether adder may keep it for you. That tick is never set for you. Without it, adder doesn’t open. You can take that consent back at any time by leaving adder (see below).

adder is for people of 18 and older.

Where it lives

Everything you add to adder is stored in Ireland. A few services help us run it:

ServiceWhat it does, what it receives, where
SupabaseOur database and login. Receives everything listed above. Servers in Ireland (American company).
ResendSends your login code and invitations. Receives the name and email address of the person receiving the mail. Servers in Ireland (American company).
ExpoDelivers app updates to your phone. Receives technical details of your phone and app version, no personal content. United States.
AppleTestFlight, the App Store and the map. Receives what Apple’s own privacy policy describes, plus crash reports and feedback you choose to send through TestFlight. United States.
Photon (OpenStreetMap)Looks up a street when you add a place. Receives only the address you type. Germany.

Where a service is American, it is bound by the EU’s standard rules for data that leaves Europe. We would rather run adder fully in Europe, and we are working on it.

Leaving adder

You can leave at any time, in the app: My Profile → Delete account. What you trusted also lives in your friends’ nests, so you choose what happens to it:

  1. Keep my place warm for a year. Your account and login are removed. Your places and words stay for your friends, without your name. We keep a scrambled key of your email address, which can’t be read back. Come back with the same email within 12 months and your name, places and friends return. After 12 months the key is erased automatically.
  2. Leave my words, without my name. Your account and login are removed. Your places and words stay for your friends, without your name. Nothing is kept to find you back.
  3. Take everything with me. Your account, login and everything you added are removed.

In all three cases your favorites are erased. Can’t get into the app any more? Mail ssst@adder.care from the address you used and we’ll do it for you.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see what we keep about you, to correct it, to receive a copy, or to erase it. Mail ssst@adder.care. We answer within a month, usually much sooner.

Not happy with how we handle it? You can file a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données), dataprotectionauthority.be.

When something in this statement changes, we tell you in the app first.
